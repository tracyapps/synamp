Accent discipline

At most two visible uses of gold per screen — typically one eyebrow and one primary action. The spectrum appears only in the amp light and the visualizer, never as a page wash or on body text.

Library care & integrity

Type rules

ALL CAPS always carries +0.06em or more. Latin display tightens to −0.02em; CJK stays at 0 tracking with line-height 1.3–1.4, including cover titles. Never fake italics — Momo has one weight and no oblique, so emphasis is colour and weight.

Buttons

One primary action per viewport. Hover moves the background, never the label toward the background. Minimum touch target is 44px.

Logo

A spectrum brain with a music note, beside a coral-to-gold wordmark. The mark and the wordmark are separate files (synamp-mark.svg, synamp-wordmark.svg) so either can change on its own; synamp-logo.svg is the two together. Use the mark alone for favicons and app tiles.

  • Clear space: x on all sides, where x is one quarter of the icon height.
  • Minimum size: icon 16px; horizontal lockup 120px wide.
  • On busy photography, use the icon over a scrim — never place the wordmark directly on a mid-tone image.
  • Never recolour the brain. The wordmark may run solid ink for single-colour embossing.

Accessibility

Focus & keyboard

Every focusable element shows a 2px gold focus ring with a 2px offset. The full journey — nav, tabs, accordions, forms, dialogs — is keyboard operable; dialogs trap focus natively and close on Escape.

Motion & data

The visualizer never autoplays sound, and stays still under prefers-reduced-motion, Save-Data or a slow connection, with an explicit opt-in. All transitions collapse to near-zero under reduced-motion.

Targets & text

Interactive targets are 44px minimum. Body text never drops below 15px on mobile; nothing relies on colour alone — status always pairs a dot with a word.

Structure

Landmarks, a skip link, table captions, labelled inputs, descriptive alt text and aria-selected/aria-current state keep the page legible to assistive tech.
