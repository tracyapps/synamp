Three steps

Installing it.

  1. Open the download and drag SynAmp into Applications. Apple has checked the app, so it opens like any other.
  2. Open SynAmp and choose the folder your music is in: on the Mac, on a drive plugged into it, or on a NAS. Nothing in that folder is changed without asking you first.
  3. Look for the waveform in the menu bar. Its menu says in plain words what SynAmp is doing, and has “Check for updates…”. SynAmp listens to each song once before it can build playlists by sound, and that takes a while on a big library. You can play music straight away.
Before you install

What to expect from a test version.

What works today

Playing your library, playlists described in plain words, library tidying with a preview and undo, radio, visuals, party mode and listening away from home. The roadmap shows each piece.

What's rough

The very first run on a Mac that has never had SynAmp is the least tested part. The first listen-through downloads one extra piece. Windows isn't here yet.

Your files are safe

SynAmp never deletes music. Renames and tidy-ups show a preview first and can be undone. See what leaves your house.

Found something broken or confusing? In SynAmp, choose Send feedback, or write to us here. Running SynAmp on a NAS or home server instead? The files for that are on the release page, and the help centre walks through it.
