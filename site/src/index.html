Why SynAmp exists

Streaming was convenient. It was never yours.

You already own the music. What went missing was the library, the playlists that fit your taste, and the joy of a player that reacts to the sound. SynAmp brings all three back — on hardware you control.

The subscription trap

Renting music means losing it the month you stop paying. SynAmp plays your own files, from your own home, whether or not the internet is having a good day.

Four thousand artist folders

“Dave Brubeck” and “Brubeck, Dave” staring back at you. SynAmp suggests tidy names, finds the tracks that went missing, and never changes a file until you say yes.

Playlists that never learn

A regular playlist can't answer “something to focus to, no words, no piano, a bit more energy”. SynAmp listens to your music as sound, not just labels.

What it does

Everything a music system should be — in one place.

SynAmp works with the phone and desktop music apps you may already use, so playing your library is polished from day one. Then it adds the parts nothing else has.

The Brain

It listens to every song for tempo, energy, mood and instruments. Ask for a playlist in your own words, and see why each song made the cut.

Playlists that scale

Folders, nesting, drag and drop, and playlists of playlists that shuffle a whole folder at once. Smooth even with a hundred thousand songs.

Library care

Find missing tracks, tidy messy names, set aside second copies, and add new music by dragging it in. Every change can be undone.

Visualizers & radio

Milkdrop-style visuals are back, along with thousands of radio stations from around the world — searchable by country, genre and mood.

DJ mode

No gaps between tracks, crossfades you can tune, and songs lined up by tempo and key so the mix keeps flowing — even when you skip mid-song.

Any device, your server

Your phone, your car and your computer can all play your library, at home or away. Later: a menu-bar player and a party mode for the big screen.

The Brain

Describe what you want to hear.

No single label captures a mood. SynAmp listens for tempo, key, energy, instruments and more, then turns your sentence into a playlist — and shows its working, so you always know why a song is there.

  • sounds like these two songs
  • no words, no piano
  • not too fast
  • songs about new beginnings

Love a song, skip it, or take it out of a playlist, and SynAmp remembers — only for that playlist, unless you say otherwise.

Visualizers

It still knows how to dance.

Remember Milkdrop? The visuals are back, plus new ones of our own. They calm down for anyone who prefers less motion, go easy on mobile data, and never make a sound until you press play.

How it fits together

Your music never leaves home.

SynAmp runs on a NAS or home server you already own. Your files stay there; you listen from anywhere over a private connection, and the heavy listening work happens on your computer so the server stays quick.
