What won't change

Promises that come with every phase.

Features will come and go on this list. These won't.

Your files, your call

SynAmp suggests; you decide. Nothing in your library changes until you approve it, and every change can be undone.

Private by default

It runs in your home, on your hardware. No account to make, no tracking, and nothing about your listening leaves the house unless you ask.

Honest playlists

Every song shows why it's there. When SynAmp isn't sure — say, whether a track has piano — it tells you instead of guessing.

Works with what you have

Keep using the phone and car apps you like. SynAmp fits around them instead of asking you to switch.

For everyone

Usable with a keyboard, a screen reader or a phone, with motion and sound that never surprise you.
