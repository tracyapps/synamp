Corrections and disagreements

The original report is a historical edition. Read these alongside it; archived claims and past test counts are not silently upgraded.

  • Original reports are historical. Autoclaw captures describe its frozen feature commit. Current checks belong to a separate receipt; prior counts are not current claims.
  • Negative proposal acceptance. The original H2/brief wording promises exclusion. The code applies a bounded global thumbs-down score; maintained product copy now says lower preference.
  • Memory statistic. Janata 2007 reports a study-specific 30% average; Janata 2009 selected participants with at least 30% in earlier validation. The maintained dossier attribution is corrected; current source access was indexed primary-text excerpts, with direct full-text opening blocked.
  • Artist gate disagreement. A3 prose says at least two distinct tracks; P17 and epoch-v1 count events. Keep the implemented rule until a versioned decision supported by evidence.
  • Dayparts and resets. Frozen B2 decisions/code supersede A3 daypart ranges and missing-session semantics. A reset with non-epoch scope is ignored; remove with not_now is epoch-only.
  • Preliminary chapter state. W1 source preamble says sequencing notes are unrendered; H2 added them. W2 cites an older lab hash; final H4 capture supersedes it.
  • Cross-session wording. Proposals are the consent channel for inferred patterns. Explicit loves, thumbs and removes can also persist at their declared scope.
  • Rollback and smoke paths. Archived reset --hard instructions apply to its feature branch, not current main. Archived .cluster smoke paths lived outside this repository. Use the maintained follow-up and scratch route checks.
  • Unsupported fields are not free. Declared neutral soft terms dilute score gaps and can alter bounded feedback/MMR selection. Floor normalization is neutral only within a pure bundle, apart from truncation; mixed plans reweight.
  • Candidate evidence. Weights, bands, proposal thresholds and fixed arcs are hypotheses. Synthetic fixtures prove invariants, not classifier accuracy, usefulness or cultural generality.
