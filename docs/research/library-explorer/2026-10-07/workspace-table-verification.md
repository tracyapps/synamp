## Evidence boundaries and follow-up

Browser screenshots and actions use an isolated five-track fixture, loopback Brain/static servers, scratch events/playlists/session stores, and synthetic radio metadata. Queue actions verify IDs/UI, not playable audio. The final desktop geometry sample begins with an existing scratch queue; the raw initial script used an “idle” label in an earlier run, which is not a claim about that sample's playback state. Separate real-library checks read the export once without changing it. Single-Mac timings exclude browser rendering, network, audio delivery and NAS/device performance.

Two-touch input is trusted Chromium CDP simulation. Physical trackpad/touch devices, screen readers and the owner's current Brave/NAS build were not exercised. Early geometry checks exposed hidden-text overflow, a zero-height landscape scene with an expanded queue, and shrinking player rows; those were corrected and fresh checks passed. A wheel-anchor check initially used fractional pointer coordinates despite Chromium delivering integer wheel coordinates; its input was corrected before the passing check.

Current inspection is one closable, content-height floating artist card. Simultaneous planet-pinned cards, connectors, collision handling, saved card arrangements, subtle motion, nested visual branches, collaboration paths and the other larger ideas remain in LE03–LE10 with dependencies and acceptance checks. This slice does not claim them implemented.

Operational receipts, library provenance, local paths and private samples must stay outside a future public knowledge-base export. Raw browser records are retained under evidence/workspace-table/browser-records. Owned scratch browser and servers were closed after verification; no live analysis was restarted.
