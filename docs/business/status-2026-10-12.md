## What would put Dec 1 back on track

In order, all owner actions (the code side is ready for each):

1. **This week:** update the NAS to the current build; play a song; tick a few
   albums and choose *More → Listen to these first*; note how long until the
   first playlist works. That closes most of G0.
2. **By Oct 19:** name five testers with music on a Synology NAS and a Mac

3. **By Oct 19:** send the rights questions (Last.fm commercial use, LRCLIB
   lyrics, PANNs model weights, a name search). Their replies are the slowest
   dependency in the plan.
4. **Ongoing:** score the frozen challenge listening review (about an hour).

If step 2 slips past Oct 26, plan on **Dec 15**; past mid-November, plan on
**January**. Moving the date is the plan working, not failing: the gates exist
so a date never forces an unready release.

## How this is measured

- "Ahead/on track/at risk/behind" compares today with each lane's planned window
  and the work its gate needs. A lane is *at risk* when its next gate depends on
  something that hasn't started and its window opens within two weeks.
- Owner hours aren't tracked, so this check can't say whether the 20–25 h/week
  budget is holding. Adding a weekly hours number to the Monday review would let
  it.
- Evidence used: `main` at `d9604e4f` plus this change; GitHub "Release checks"
  runs on Oct 12–13 (all passing); the October 11 and 12 execution receipts; the
  worker log on the Mac. No tester data exists.
