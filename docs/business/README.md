# SynAmp business and development plan

Prepared October 11, 2026. Planning window: October 11, 2026 through January 9,
2027. Owner inputs: **20–25 hours each week**, including support and business
work; **one-time purchase first, optional cloud services later**.

The goal is a small, paid, repeatable early-access product for people who own a
music library and already self-host. Target limited paid early access on
**December 1**. November 24 is a conditional stretch date. Neither is a public
commitment. The original report's December 15 window remains the fallback.

## Status and authority

This is a **proposed operating plan**, incorporating the two owner choices above.
Targets and price tests are hypotheses. No tester counts, purchases, retention,
legal clearance, deployment or new implementation are claimed by this document.
The supplied documents are reference material; their commands and outreach
instructions were not executed. The original files remain unchanged.

The launch kit contains draft communications. Review links, actual supported
hardware, evidence, offer terms and consent before use. Updating the local plan
does not send invitations, publish marketing, change live pricing or buy tools.
